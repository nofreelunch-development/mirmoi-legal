This policy explains what data the Mirmoi app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the iOS and Android versions of the app.

## What we collect
- **Anonymous account identifier.** When the app first starts we create a random, anonymous user ID (via Supabase) so your purchases can be restored across devices. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple/Google account.
- **Purchase and subscription data.** Product IDs, purchase receipts/tokens, and subscription status are validated and stored by RevenueCat on our behalf to unlock features and restore purchases.
- **Advertising data (Google AdMob).** Device and app identifiers (for example the vendor identifier, or the advertising ID where you have permitted it), ad impressions and clicks, anti-fraud signals, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
- **Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics).** Crash logs, stack traces, and device/app context to improve stability.
- **Support communications** if you email us.
- **On-device data.** Favorites, preferences, and reminder times are stored locally on your device. Reminders are scheduled on-device; nothing about them is sent to us.

## What we do NOT collect
We do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS/call logs, photos, health data, or financial credentials. We do not sell personal data.

## How we use data
- Provide app functionality and unlock the features you have paid for
- Show ads to free users and measure ad performance
- Restore purchases and prevent fraud
- Improve reliability and respond to support requests

## Advertising
Ads are served by Google AdMob. On iOS we do not ask for tracking permission, so ads are non-personalised unless you have allowed tracking system-wide. You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings:

- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
- **Android:** Settings → Google → Ads

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

Subscribers to a paid plan do not see ads.

## Third-party services
We use the following services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

- **Google AdMob** — ads and ad measurement
- **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) — crash reporting
- **RevenueCat** — subscription validation and management
- **Supabase** — anonymous accounts and entitlement records
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — payments and subscription billing

## Retention and deletion
- **Purchase records** are kept for as long as needed to honour your subscription and for legal and accounting purposes (up to 7 years).
- **Crash diagnostics** are retained by Firebase for 90 days.
- **Advertising data** is retained by Google according to its policies.
- **On-device data** is removed when you uninstall the app.

To delete the data associated with your anonymous ID, email **support@mirmoi.com** with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and include the User ID shown in the app’s Settings screen. We will respond within 30 days.

## Your choices
- Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings
- Limit ad personalisation using the device settings above
- Request deletion of your data as described above

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We do not use custom encryption.

We may update this policy and will update the date above. Material changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
